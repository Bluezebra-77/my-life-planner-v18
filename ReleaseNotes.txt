My Life Planner v51b — Internal Release Notes

NEW
- Monthly recurring tasks can repeat on the same calendar date or on the first, second, third, fourth or last chosen weekday.
- Rules can repeat every N months, such as every two months on the second Thursday.
- Existing monthly recurring tasks remain same-date rules unless edited.
- Tags can be entered on to-dos, projects, annual dates, cleaning tasks, appointments, recurring tasks, Brain Inbox and Waiting For items.
- Tags are displayed as chips and participate in Lists search.

FIXED
- The Home Brain Inbox microphone and Show/Hide controls are separated and no longer overlap on narrow screens.

PRESERVED
- Existing v51a migration, backup and rollback infrastructure.
- lifePlannerData and Today's Focus safety mirror.
- Daily Rhythm customisation and all accepted v50/v51a behaviour.
