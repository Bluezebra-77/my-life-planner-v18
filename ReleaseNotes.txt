My Life Planner
Version 46
Date: 31 July 2026

BUILT FROM
- Version 45 supplied by the user after acceptance testing.

STABILITY FIXES
- Custom-list editor now keeps item text visible alongside Complete and Delete controls on iPhone.
- Lists side navigation is constrained to the visual viewport and can no longer widen the page and trigger Safari page shrinking.
- Home task text opens the task; completion remains a separate circular control.
- Header progress now includes saved to-do completion as well as Daily Rhythm and evening checks, and refreshes immediately.
- Lists search now filters rendered to-dos, appointments, Brain Inbox, Waiting For, annual dates, projects, cleaning tasks and custom lists.
- Random task choices now use user-saved tasks only; built-in examples such as Vinted and “Sort one small box” are excluded.

DATA COMPATIBILITY
- Existing localStorage keys and database format remain unchanged.
- Existing tasks, projects, appointments, custom lists, Brain Inbox items and settings remain readable.

DEFERRED TO LATER STAGES
- Reordering project steps.
- Advanced monthly weekday recurrence.
- Early appointment visibility and notifications.
- Broader global search and navigation improvements.
