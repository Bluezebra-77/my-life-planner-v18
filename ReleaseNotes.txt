My Life Planner
Version 45
Date: 30 July 2026

BUILT FROM
- Last user-tested stable release: v42

NEW
- Automatic image resizing/compression for Brain Inbox images.
- In-app image, PDF and text previews where supported.
- Quick Actions with a generated Brain Inbox launch address.
- Flexible appointment recurrence: every X days, weeks, months or years.
- Repeat endings: never, after a chosen number, or on a chosen date.
- Developer Dashboard showing release, module, data-count and storage information.
- ReleaseNotes.txt and BuildReport.txt included in the package.

IMPROVED
- Photo and screenshot capture combined as Photo or image.
- Clear preview versus download behaviour for attachments.
- Version consistency across app header, About, JavaScript, manifest and service-worker cache.

DATA COMPATIBILITY
- Existing localStorage keys are unchanged.
- Existing appointments and Brain Inbox records remain readable.
- Existing non-repeating and legacy repeating appointments remain supported.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Some document types cannot be previewed by Safari and will use Download copy.
- Browser storage allowance varies by device and browser.
