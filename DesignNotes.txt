MY LIFE PLANNER — DESIGN NOTES

CORE PRINCIPLES
- One item has one logical home; other screens are views.
- Do not add a second route that merely duplicates Timeline.
- Existing user data is preserved through every internal and public release.
- Internal lettered milestones are test builds; public versions remain whole numbers.

HOME AND TIMELINE
- Home answers: what needs attention now?
- Timeline answers: what happens over time?
- Dashboard additions must provide clear value, not duplicate Timeline filters.

SEARCH
- Partial terms and tags remain supported.
- Within each list section, stronger matches should sort above weaker matches.
