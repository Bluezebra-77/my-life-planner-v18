MY LIFE PLANNER — DESIGN NOTES

CORE
- Capture first, organise later, improve over time.
- One item has one logical home; other screens are useful views.
- Home should show what matters now, not explain the application.

PLANNER HEALTH
- Show one useful suggestion at a time.
- Suggestions must be dismissible and snoozable.
- Avoid nagging and gamification.
- Project inactivity is a prompt to review, not a judgement.

PROGRESS BAR
- Retained temporarily; review after extended real-world usage.

SETTINGS DESIGN RULE
- Settings should reveal detail on demand. Major areas remain collapsed unless the user opens them, and the planner remembers that choice.
