MY LIFE PLANNER — DESIGN NOTES

CORE PRINCIPLES
- One item has one logical home; other screens are views.
- Existing user data is preserved through every internal and public release.
- Internal lettered milestones are test builds; public versions remain whole numbers.

RECURRING TASKS
- Obligations remain due until completed.
- Same-date monthly recurrence retains the intended day where possible.
- Weekday-position recurrence is anchored to the selected ordinal and weekday, such as second Thursday.
- Late completion advances to the first matching occurrence after today, not from the completion date.

TAGS
- Tags are lightweight labels stored on the item.
- Input is comma-separated, trimmed and de-duplicated case-insensitively.
- Tags are visible and searchable; they do not move the item from its logical home.

DATA SAFETY
- v51a migration, backup, validation and rollback framework remains the foundation.
