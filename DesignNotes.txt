MY LIFE PLANNER — DESIGN NOTES

CORE PRINCIPLES
- The planner adapts to how the user lives.
- One item has one logical home; other screens are views.
- Real-world usage drives development.
- Upgrades must not discard user data.

RECURRING BEHAVIOUR
- Appointments happen at a time and may advance automatically.
- Recurring tasks remain overdue until completed.
- Their schedule remains anchored to the intended recurrence.

MIGRATION ARCHITECTURE — v51a
- lifePlannerData remains authoritative.
- Every saved record carries schemaVersion.
- Migrations run in ordered, one-version steps.
- A snapshot is created before any schema change.
- Core collections are validated after migration.
- Diagnostics are retained locally; failed work leaves a rollback snapshot.
- Today's Focus remains additionally mirrored in lifePlannerTodayFocus.
