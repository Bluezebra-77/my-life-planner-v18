MY LIFE PLANNER — DESIGN NOTES

TODAY'S FOCUS
One-off daily intentions should not require dates, reminders or permanent task records. Entry must be rapid. Incomplete items carry forward; completed items remain visible until removed so mistakes can be reversed.

HOME SCREEN
Home is a dashboard for what needs attention now. Every substantial section is collapsible and remembers its state so the user controls how much detail is visible.

PLANNER
Planner remains the time-based view. Duplication between Home and Planner will be reviewed after v48 is tested with real data.

PROJECTS
Project names and progress summaries are visible first. Steps use progressive disclosure and appear only when the project is expanded or a search matches a step.

CUSTOM LISTS
Completed items are not immediately destroyed. They move below active items, are struck through, and can be reinstated. Deletion remains a deliberate Manage action.

RECURRENCE
Calendar appointments occur and advance. Recurring tasks remain due/overdue until explicitly completed. This distinction is planned for the Smart Planner release.
