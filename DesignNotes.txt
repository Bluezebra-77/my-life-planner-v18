MY LIFE PLANNER — DESIGN NOTES

CORE PRINCIPLES
- The planner adapts to how the user lives.
- Real-world usage drives development.
- One item has one logical home; other screens are views, not duplicate records.
- The 30-Second Rule: a quick thought should be quick to capture.
- Keep screens calm through progressive disclosure.

HOME PROJECT BEHAVIOUR
- Home is a working dashboard, not merely a shortcut page.
- A project title expands its existing steps.
- A step can be completed directly from Home.
- Editing/adding/reorganising remains an explicit Manage action.

SEARCH BEHAVIOUR
- Users should not need to remember exact wording.
- Search is case-insensitive and punctuation-tolerant.
- Each entered word must occur somewhere in the searchable item, but words need not be adjacent or in the saved order.
- Partial terms are accepted.

RECURRING BEHAVIOUR
- Appointments happen at a time and can advance automatically.
- Recurring tasks represent obligations and remain overdue until completed.
- The weekly injection belongs to recurring-task behaviour, planned for v50.
