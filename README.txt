My Life Planner v44

Corrective release: Timeline rendering is isolated so it cannot stop dashboard and list summaries. Timeline now accepts current and legacy date/name fields and skips malformed records safely.


v44 changes: The Lists drawer now opens at a clearly usable height, shows a visible More Lists cue only when scrolling is needed, and stays attached to the planner edge on wide MacBook screens.
v40 changes: Lists navigation now always starts closed, stays on the right on iPhone and MacBook, closes and resets on rotation or browser resizing, always includes a close button, and remains independently scrollable in landscape. All tested v39 features remain included.
v39 changes: The mobile Lists drawer now has a close button, closes when tapping outside, and uses a narrower independently scrollable right-side layout in iPhone landscape. All v38 fixes remain included.

v37 changes: Lists navigation moved to the top and the Planner header and progress area became more compact.


V44: Brain Inbox 2.0 foundation adds website links, photo/screenshot/document attachments and direct launch with ?open=brain-inbox. Attachments remain on the current device and should be kept under 1.5 MB.

V44 adds automatic image reduction, in-app image/PDF/text previews, and Settings > Quick Actions for a Brain Inbox shortcut address.

V44: Flexible appointment repeats (every X days/weeks/months/years) with never, count, or date limits.
