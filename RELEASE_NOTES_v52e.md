# My Life Planner v52e — Cleaning by Area

- Room or area now powers a Cleaning-list filter.
- Area shortcut chips show live task counts.
- Optional Group by room view organises jobs under room headings.
- Tasks without an area appear under Unassigned.
- The chosen filter and grouping preference are remembered on this device.
- Notification support is recorded as a future roadmap item; it is not included in this build.
