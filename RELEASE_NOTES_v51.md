# My Life Planner v51 - Release Notes

**Release date:** 31 July 2026

v51 consolidates the accepted v51a-v51f development milestones into one whole-number production release.

## Highlights

- Safe data migrations with automatic pre-migration backup, validation and rollback diagnostics
- Advanced recurring tasks, including every-N periods and first/second/third/fourth/last weekday monthly rules
- Tags on core planner items and tag-aware search
- Today's Focus working area with notes, estimates, pinning, ordering and persistent focus timer
- Convert a quick item into a To-do, Cleaning Task, Recurring Task, Appointment, Project or Waiting For item
- Quick Add from Home, Lists and Planner
- Planner Health phase 1 with one calm suggestion at a time, plus snooze and dismiss memory
- Home reordered around the day's priorities, with explanatory information available on demand
- Waiting For and Gentle close-down restored to Home
- Major Settings areas independently collapsible with remembered state
- Mobile fixes for list actions, timer positioning and Quick Add clearance

## Data compatibility

Existing v50 and v51 test-build data is preserved. The primary storage key remains `lifePlannerData`.

## Open design reviews

- Continue evaluating whether the progress bar provides useful motivation or information
- Continue reviewing the distinction between Home and Timeline through real-life use
- Generated-task timer remains a future enhancement
