# My Life Planner v53a — Workflow & Stability

## Restored and corrected
- Today — time-sensitive is restored to the top of the working Home sections.
- Show/Hide controls and remembered panel states are restored.
- Due and overdue recurring tasks now appear in Today.
- Home recurring tasks can be edited directly.
- iPhone section headings are smaller and controls wrap without covering titles.
- Cleaning Room/Area selection, remembered suggestions, filters and grouping are preserved.
- Flexible recurrence supports every-N days/weeks/months/years and first/second/third/fourth/last weekday rules.
- Project steps open their full editor when selected from Lists/search; completion remains available in the menu.
- Evening Reflection and Hidden Statistics use the same activity history.
- Gentle close-down resets automatically on first opening of a new day and also has a manual Reset button.
- Waiting For, birthdays and all main save/edit workflows remain based on the accepted v53 Corrected baseline.

## Preserved
Convert-as-Move, Daily Companion, Planner Health, reflections, Cleaning by Area, smart defaults, search shortcuts and all established storage keys.
