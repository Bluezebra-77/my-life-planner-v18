DESIGN PRINCIPLES
- Real-world usage drives the roadmap.
- One piece of information has one logical home; other screens are views, not duplicates.
- The 30-Second Rule: a quick thought must be quick to capture.
- The planner adapts to the user's life, not the other way around.
- Keep screens calm through progressive disclosure and collapsible detail.
- Home answers: “What needs my attention now?”
- Planner answers: “What is happening over time?”
