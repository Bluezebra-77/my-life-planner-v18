# My Life Planner v52b — The Intelligent Planner

## Planner Health 2.0
- Calmer project, Waiting For and cleaning prompts.
- Older Brain Inbox items are grouped into one considerate review suggestion.
- One suggestion is shown at a time with snooze and dismiss controls.

## Repeated-task learning
- From v52b onward, repeated Today’s Focus wording is counted locally on the device.
- After three entries, Planner Health may offer to pre-fill a recurring task.
- Nothing is created automatically.
