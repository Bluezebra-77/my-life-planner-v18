# My Life Planner v52f — Polish Release

## Interface consistency
- Standardised minimum sizes for frequently used controls and improved compact mobile spacing.
- Added friendlier, more informative empty states.
- Added reduced-motion support and a keyboard-accessible skip link.

## Smarter forms
- Cleaning Room or Area now suggests rooms already used in the planner.
- The most recently used cleaning room is offered as the next default.
- The most recently used recurring-task period is remembered as a starting point.

## Search and keyboard
- Lists search is lightly debounced for smoother typing with larger datasets.
- Press `/` from outside a form to open Lists search.
- `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + F` also opens Lists search.
- Escape clears and closes the search field.

## Preserved
All v52e data, reflections, Planner Health, Daily Companion, Cleaning by Area, recurrence, timer, Quick Add and backup behaviour are preserved.
