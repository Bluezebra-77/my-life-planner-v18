# My Life Planner v53 — Corrected Workflow Edition

- Built directly from the accepted v52f baseline.
- Preserves the accepted Home order with Today first, followed by Needs Attention and Today's Focus.
- Preserves collapsible Home sections and remembered Show/Hide states.
- Preserves Waiting For menus, Birthday saving and all accepted v52f forms and workflows.
- Today’s Focus Convert now behaves as Move: the source is removed only after a successful destination save.
- Cancelling a conversion leaves the original Focus item untouched.
- The centred heading presentation is retained.
- Notifications remain a future roadmap item and are not claimed in this release.
