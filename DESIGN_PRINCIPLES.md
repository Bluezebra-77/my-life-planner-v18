# Design Principles

1. **Capture first, organise later, improve over time.**
2. **One item has one logical home; other screens are useful views.**
3. **Home shows what matters now rather than explaining the application.**
4. **Real-life use drives development.**
5. **Information appears on demand instead of permanently occupying space.**
6. **Recurring responsibilities remain visible until completed.**
7. **Suggestions are helpful, dismissible and non-judgemental.**
8. **Public releases use whole numbers; lettered builds are internal test milestones.**
9. **No release is accepted without data-retention and regression testing.**
10. **If a feature adds more friction or duplication than value, simplify or remove it.**
