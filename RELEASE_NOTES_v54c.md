# My Life Planner v54cR — Recurring Limits and Home Previews

- Recurring tasks can continue indefinitely, end on a chosen date, or stop after a chosen number of occurrences.
- Finished limited recurring tasks become inactive rather than being deleted.
- Home Brain Inbox and Recurring Tasks previews show three items initially and provide Show all / Show less.
- Brain Inbox preview prioritises the oldest unprocessed captures.
- A recurring task advanced to a future date appears immediately in Home → This Week as well as Timeline.
- Existing v54b Smart Projects and Project Templates remain preserved.
