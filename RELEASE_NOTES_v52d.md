# My Life Planner v52d — Daily Companion Part 2

## Added
- Optional Evening Reflection after 6pm.
- Configurable Weekly Reflection on Sunday evening or Monday morning.
- Gentle, non-judgemental encouragement.
- Planner Memory 2.0, based on completion patterns recorded from v52d onward.
- Hidden Statistics in Settings.

## Privacy and behaviour
- Activity is stored locally on the device.
- Nothing is scheduled automatically.
- Reflections can be hidden and statistics remain out of Home.
- Existing planner data and v52c features are preserved.
