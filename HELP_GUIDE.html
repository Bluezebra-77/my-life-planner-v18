My Life Planner Help Centre

Use this page when you need a reminder. The planner keeps explanations out of the working screens so your tasks remain the focus.

Start the day

Check Today for fixed and dated commitments, then Needs Attention, followed by the practical one-off items in Today’s Focus.

Capture first

Use Quick Add or Brain Inbox when you need to record something quickly. Use Convert later when you know where it belongs.

Planner Health

Planner Health shows one considerate suggestion at a time. You can open it, snooze it for seven days or dismiss it. It never changes your records automatically.

Repeating tasks

Use Recurring Tasks for responsibilities that remain due until completed. Cleaning Tasks automatically calculate their next due date after completion.

Protect your data

Use Settings → Backup and restore to create a backup file regularly, especially before a major update.
